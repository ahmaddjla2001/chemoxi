Who We Are

CHEMOXI LLC is a science-driven chemical company focused on research, formulation, application development, and product optimization. We combine chemistry knowledge with practical product-development strategies to help turn concepts into usable, scalable formulations.

Our work spans cleaning and hygiene products, surfactant systems, ingredient substitution, process optimization, specialty formulations, and laboratory-to-production development.
